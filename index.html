How to Add Manually

  1. Open your F-Droid client app (F-Droid, Droid-ify, or Neo Store).
  2. Go to Settings → Repositories (or Manage Repositories).
  3. Tap + or Add Repository.
  4. Paste the address: https://fdroid.pixelabs.in/repo
  5. Paste the Fingerprint: 3C3E9EF8F74E8E057FA0EA4965D19DE1E6A223719D0B2D6870FDC59F6B20B55E
  6. Tap Add and synchronize!
